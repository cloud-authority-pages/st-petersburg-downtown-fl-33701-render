Bed Bug Treatment Options After Inspection Confirmation

Following a bed bug dog inspection confirms an active infestation, property owners have two primary professional paths: heat treatment and chemical applications. Chemical treatments use a rotation of EPA-approved products applied to cracks, crevices, and other hiding spots identified during the inspection. Because resistance varies by population, technicians often combine multiple active ingredients and follow up with a second visit to address any eggs that hatched after the first application. Some properties benefit from a hybrid approach where heat is used in the most heavily infested rooms and residual products protect adjacent spaces.
